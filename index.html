The Art of
Slow Distillation

Modern spray fragrances are up to 90% synthetic alcohol, evaporating into thin air within hours. Our Ittars are crafted using the ancient hydro-distillation method.

Slow-boiled in sealed copper stills over clay ovens, the precious steam condensed into a base of pure, sustainably sourced Indian Sandalwood oil.

We age our elixirs in dark, obsidian glass jars for a minimum of 18 months, allowing the fragrance molecules to bind, mature, and unlock unparalleled depth.
